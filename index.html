RESEARCH-GRADE REFERENCE IMPLEMENTATION
This demonstrates how high-risk AI systems could be governed, audited, and operationalized in democratic contexts.

NOT: a live system, NOT connected to classified data, NOT an official deployment.

CORE INNOVATIONS:

  • AI Restraint Index: Single score (uncertainty + bias + harm + legal exposure). When >0.60: advisory-only mode, block escalation.
  • Legal Bundles: Cryptographically sealed evidence (inputs, models, humans, alternatives, policy). Court-ready.
  • Decision Context: Trigger source, data provenance, uncertainty all visible.
  • Restraint Indicator: Real-time system state, confidence, bias warnings, conflicts.
  • Action Logs: Role-based approval chains, hashed IDs (privacy + accountability).
  • Live Threat Detection: Multimodal fusion + fairness parity calculation, integrated in-dashboard.

WHY INDIA CAN LEAD: Active judiciary + RTI culture + DPDP enforcement needs = perfect conditions.
