⚡ Dual-Use Value Framework
This analysis enables: (1) Early humanitarian crisis warning (floods, displacement) for resource pre-positioning, and (2) Smart borders for proportionate enforcement. Both security and humanitarian analytical streams run in parallel from the same sensor data. When these streams reach conflicting conclusions — as in T-2847 — the conflict is treated as a mandatory escalation trigger.
Humanitarian Parallel Channel — How It Operates
›
The humanitarian parallel channel is a structurally independent advisory stream that runs alongside the security operations stream. It is staffed by humanitarian coordinators with UNHCR data sharing access and operates under a separate mandate and reporting line. Its outputs are not filtered by the security chain of command before reaching the AI Governance Officer. For T-2847, the parallel channel assessment is: high probability civilian displacement movement — recommend non-interception response and referral to protection services.

All humanitarian data is processed in accordance with UNHCR data protection standards and the Digital SORA framework for refugee data. Biometric data of registered refugees is not retained beyond the immediate operational window.
⚖ Fairness Audit vs Protection Mandate — Conflict Resolution Principle
›
The fairness dashboard tracks demographic parity — equalising false positive rates across nationality groups. The humanitarian channel operates under protection mandates — non-refoulement, access to asylum, proportionate response. These two frameworks make different and sometimes conflicting claims about what the system owes to the people it classifies.

When they conflict: If achieving demographic parity would require reducing detection sensitivity in a corridor where an active humanitarian protection assessment exists, the protection mandate takes precedence. The fairness correction is paused, the conflict is logged as a governance exception, and the Governance Officer is notified.

Why: Demographic parity is a statistical property of model outputs. Non-refoulement is a legal obligation with individual consequences. They are not equivalent constraints. A system that trades off protection mandates to optimise parity scores has misunderstood what fairness requires in this context.

Audit trail: All governance exceptions of this type are logged with the specific parity score, the active protection assessment, the corridor, and the officer who acknowledged the conflict. This log is included in the Legal Bundle.

◆ Scoring Methodology — How the composite is calculated
›
Composite formula: Weighted average of four sub-scores.

Weights: Civilian Harm 0.40 · Uncertainty 0.25 · Legal Exposure 0.20 · Bias Risk 0.15

Calculation for this incident: (0.40 × 0.40) + (0.25 × 0.22) + (0.20 × 0.23) + (0.15 × 0.18) = 0.160 + 0.055 + 0.046 + 0.027 = 0.288 (raw). Scaled to 0–1 range with threshold normalisation: composite 0.73.

Weight rationale: Civilian Harm carries the highest weight because potential for physical harm to people is lexically prior — it cannot be traded off against lower uncertainty or legal compliance. This is a governance choice, not a technical one. All weight adjustments are logged and require Governance Officer sign-off.

Threshold: 0.60. Above this score, the system enters advisory-only mode. The threshold is a configurable governance parameter — any change requires documented rationale and is logged in the audit trail.

About This Architecture
Design principles · Governance frameworks · Reference implementation

PRAVAAH C4I is a research-grade reference implementation exploring how high-risk AI systems could be governed, audited, and operationalized where national security mandates and humanitarian obligations meet. The core argument: that dual-use AI governance frameworks — where the same sensor data serves both security and protection purposes — require accountability architectures that don't yet exist in policy, and this is what one would look like.

It is NOT a live system, NOT connected to classified data, and NOT an official deployment. All operational data is illustrative and does not represent real intelligence, real incidents, or real individuals. The architecture is built entirely through AI-assisted development — demonstrating that domain knowledge and governance instincts, not traditional coding, are the core competencies required to build meaningful AI governance tools.

Core Innovations

  • AI Restraint Index: Transparent composite score (civilian harm × 0.40 + uncertainty × 0.25 + legal exposure × 0.20 + bias risk × 0.15). When >0.60: advisory-only mode, block auto-escalation. Formula and weights are visible and auditable.
  • Fairness-Humanitarian Conflict Resolution: When demographic parity optimisation conflicts with an active protection mandate, the protection mandate takes precedence. Conflicts are logged as governance exceptions — not silently resolved.
  • Legal Bundles: Cryptographically sealed evidence packages (inputs, models, humans, alternatives, policy context). Court-ready.
  • Decision Context Panels: Trigger source, data provenance, and uncertainty all visible to operators in plain language.
  • Dual-Use Value Framework: Security and humanitarian analytical streams run in parallel from the same sensor data.
  • Humanitarian Parallel Channel: Structurally independent of the security chain of command — cannot be suppressed by operational pressure.
  • Demographic Fairness Auditing: Parity tracking across nationality groups, bias flag registration, geographic correction coefficients.
  • Role-Based Action Logs: Pseudonymized hashed IDs preserve privacy while maintaining full accountability chains.

Governance Frameworks Applied

Why India Can Lead

Active judiciary + RTI culture + DPDP enforcement needs = conditions for cryptographic audit trails, fairness mandates, and AI accountability architecture to be adopted at scale. India's policy vacuum around refugee management is not a gap to be filled by AI — it is a governance challenge that AI systems operating in this context must be designed to navigate, not paper over.
